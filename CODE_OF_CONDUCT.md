# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a community where participation is safe, respectful, and constructive regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to correction.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish facts from preferences.
- Protect security reports, credentials, private data, and unpublished work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the outcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory, degrading, insulting, or dehumanizing language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against reporters, reviewers, maintainers, or participants.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose credentials, private prompts, employer data, or security details.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, chat spaces, events, and when someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Reports should be sent privately to `CODE_OF_CONDUCT_CONTACT_TBD`. This placeholder must be replaced with a monitored contact before public community participation begins. Until then, contact the repository owner privately through their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in an issue.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible, avoid conflicts of interest, gather relevant context, and use proportionate responses. Possible actions include a private correction, warning, temporary participation limits, removal of content, temporary suspension, or permanent removal. Retaliation and repeated behavior increase severity.

Anyone handling a report must recuse themselves when personally involved. Decisions and appeals should be reviewed by an uninvolved maintainer whenever one is available.
